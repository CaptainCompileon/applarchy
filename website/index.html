I. THE IDEA

Omarchy ships a screensaver that plays random effects from TerminalTextEffects over its logo, in a terminal.

Applarchy brings the same effects to the Mac. Not in a terminal window, but as a real macOS screensaver and a live desktop wallpaper, with the effect engine compiled right in.

Every frame is still text: the engine moves coloured characters around a grid. Applarchy draws that grid as pixel art, block characters as exact rectangles, so the logo stays crisp on any display.

II. THE EFFECTS

All 37, from the same engine Omarchy uses: beams, matrix rain, thunderstorms, fireworks, black holes, decrypt and more. Tick the ones you like; your picks play in random order, each once before any repeats.

    III. THE LOOK

    i) THEMES

    Omarchy's own colours, or eight of its themes. A theme recolours the effects, the background and the clock. Or make your own: background, clock, gradient and effect colours.

    Hover one to play it. Click to use it in the window above.

    ii) YOUR OWN LOGO

    Choose your vibe: Chill Guy, or your own logo from any picture, pasted text art or a blank canvas, touched up square by square. Every effect animates it. Logos you use are kept in My Logos, to export and share.

    Draw here: click or drag to fill squares, start on a filled one or right-click to erase.

    IV. THE MODES

    i) SMOOTH MODE

    The default. One click sets the screensaver, the lock screen and the desktop to one look. When you come back, the animation plays out and the logo glides to the centre, the same way on the lock screen and on the desktop, which carries on from where the screensaver was.

    Turn it off and your previous wallpaper and lock screen come back.

    ii) SCREENSAVER

    Appears in System Settings → Screen Saver, with an optional pixel-font clock: 12- or 24-hour or following the system, with or without the date, small or large. On every display, the same effect plays in step.

    iii) LIVE WALLPAPER

    Runs behind your desktop icons on every screen and Space, and pauses itself whenever the desktop is covered, the display sleeps or the screensaver runs. Pause it or turn it off from the storm cloud in the menu bar.

    V. UNDER THE HOOD

    The effects come from ttfx, the Rust port of TerminalTextEffects that Omarchy uses, embedded with a small C API that builds an effect on a fixed canvas and hands out frames one at a time. A native screensaver view parses each frame's cells and draws them as pixel art.

    • 120 FPS PACE

      Effects run at Omarchy's 120 fps pace whatever the frame rate. Each tick catches the engine up to where it should be, so a slow tick drops a drawn frame instead of slowing the effect.

    • CRISP, AND CHEAP

      The wallpaper draws into an 8-bit bitmap at 1× and lets the compositor scale it up nearest-neighbour: crisp pixel art at a fraction of the cost of a Retina redraw.

    • EVERY SCREEN IN STEP

      All screens play the same effect, seed and timing. The layout scales to the tighter of width or height, so ultrawide and portrait displays look like a laptop's.

    • DRAWN, NOT SHIPPED

      The logo isn't artwork in the app: it's drawn at runtime from your Mac's own system font and resampled into half-block pixels.

    • REDUCE MOTION AWARE

      With macOS's Reduce Motion on, only the calm effects play. This page does the same.

    • UNIVERSAL

      Runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 13 Ventura or later.

    VI. THE TERMS

    Free and open source under the MIT licence. No account, no subscription, no analytics.

    Applarchy makes one network request: once a day it asks GitHub's public API for the latest release, to tell you about updates. Nothing about you or your Mac is sent.

    It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first launch needs one extra click in System Settings, unless you install from Terminal. After that it opens like any other app.
